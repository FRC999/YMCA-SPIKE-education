SPIKE Prime hub lights

Identify the button, color and pattern. Display letters are separate program outputs.

ButtonColor and patternMeaning and action
CenterGreen, steadyHub is on, its OS is compatible with SPIKE, and battery is at least 20%. Still check the correct hub connection, program and clear lane before running.
CenterWhite, steadyHub OS needs an update to work with SPIKE. Ask the mentor to follow the app's update instructions.
CenterOrange, flashingBattery is below 20%. Tell the mentor and connect the USB cable to charge.
CenterRed, flashingExtended load. Stop, tell the mentor, switch the hub off and let it cool for 15 minutes.
CenterPurple, flashingFirmware update is in progress. Let the update finish and follow the app instructions.
ConnectionBlue, flashingWaiting for a Bluetooth connection. Check the intended hub name with the mentor.
ConnectionBlue, steadyConnected by Bluetooth. This is not a battery-level indication.
