# Week 6 print and bring checklist

A rescue attachment. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **Print one [22-page class packet](../../02_Student/Printouts/W06_Class_Print_Packet.pdf)** on US Letter, single-sided, Actual Size (100%) with automatic page orientation. It includes the mentor checklist, assembly guide, two complete fields, five team sheets, four build cards and attendance.

When printing individual files:

- [ ] **Two sets of pages 2–6** from the [full-size rescue field](../../02_Student/Printouts/W06_Rescue_Full_Size_Field.pdf). Each set has Week 5 tiles A–C, a 6 cm dark strip and rescue tabs. Page 1 shows assembly and where to mark the bay.
- [ ] **5 copies** of [W06_rescue_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W06_rescue_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W06_rescue_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W06_rescue_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copy** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copy** of [W06_rescue_Checklist.pdf](W06_rescue_Checklist.pdf) for per-kit sign-off.

The two field sets, student sheets and attendance take **20 sheets**. Add the guide and checklist for the complete 22-page set. Reuse role cards and hub guides.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 6 guide bases and 12 short side strips, or equivalent allocated beams and pins.
- [ ] 6 lightweight cubes: 4 at build tables + 2 at robots.
- [ ] 2 full-size rescue lanes, 2 dark strips, 2 sets of rescue tabs and 2 mentor sample guides, with sensor D clear.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 6 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W06_rescue.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Reserve equal spare parts and prepare four build packs. Join the 24 × 60 cm lanes at the paired ticks and tape the seams from behind. Set the strip's near edge at the 25 cm marks. Center sensor D at START on each lane and mark the starting cube just ahead of the fitted mentor plow.
- [ ] With the plow attached, remeasure white, seams and the dark strip and recheck each kit's Week 5 threshold. Remove strip and cube for a full run to L and verify clear runout. Then restore the strip and cube and confirm M. Mark the bay around the resting cube using the side tabs. Confirm the whole cube base can stay in the bay and repeat from the same start.
- [ ] Each robot station has a mentor sample before the first rotation. Fit approved team guides for retests. Keep motor and sensor mounts unchanged.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
