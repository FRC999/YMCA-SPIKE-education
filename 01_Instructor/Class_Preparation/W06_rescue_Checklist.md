# Week 6 print and bring checklist

A rescue attachment. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **5 copies** of [W06_rescue_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W06_rescue_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W06_rescue_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W06_rescue_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [W06_rescue_Checklist.pdf](W06_rescue_Checklist.pdf) — lead packing checklist; optional if checked digitally.

Required student/record sheets: **10 pages**. Add one optional packing checklist and one optional lead speaker-note copy. Print the reusable role cards (4 sheets) and hub guides (2 sheets) for Week 1, or replace lost copies later. Do not print the entire workbook for every child.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 6 guide bases and 12 short side strips, or equivalent allocated beams and pins.
- [ ] 6 lightweight cubes: 4 at build tables + 2 at robots.
- [ ] 2 tested marker lanes and 2 mentor sample guides, with sensor D clear.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 6 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W06_rescue.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Reserve equal spare parts for each team. Test a passive plow mount. Place the stop strip 25 cm ahead of the color sensor at the starting pose. After a dry run, mark the cube delivery zone ahead of the stopped plow. Open W06_rescue.py. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] Each robot station has a mentor sample before the first rotation. Fit approved team guides for retests. Keep motor and sensor mounts unchanged.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
