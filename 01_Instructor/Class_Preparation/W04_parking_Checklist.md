# Week 4 print and bring checklist

Parking assistant. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **5 copies** of [W04_parking_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W04_parking_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W04_parking_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W04_parking_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [W04_parking_Checklist.pdf](W04_parking_Checklist.pdf) — lead packing checklist; optional if checked digitally.

Required student/record sheets: **10 pages**. Add one optional packing checklist and one optional lead speaker-note copy. Print the reusable role cards (4 sheets) and hub guides (2 sheets) for Week 1, or replace lost copies later. Do not print the entire workbook for every child.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 6 broad flat wall panels, 12 brace strips and 6 floor panels.
- [ ] 2 distance sensors mounted on C, one per robot.
- [ ] 4 footprint proxies, one per team; 2 mentor garages ready before class.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 4 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W04_parking.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Mount the distance sensor before class. Open W04_parking.py. At 100, 150, and 250 mm, check readings against the ruler. Start about 300 mm from a broad, upright wall. Keep hands out of the lane. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] Use the mentor wall during initial parking tests. The mentor approves the team garage before its robot retest; keep the broad wall and sensor line of sight clear.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
