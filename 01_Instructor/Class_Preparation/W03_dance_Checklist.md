# Week 3 print and bring checklist

Robot dance studio. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **5 copies** of [W03_dance_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W03_dance_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W03_dance_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W03_dance_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [W03_dance_Checklist.pdf](W03_dance_Checklist.pdf) — lead packing checklist; optional if checked digitally.

Required student/record sheets: **10 pages**. Add one optional packing checklist and one optional lead speaker-note copy. Print the reusable role cards (4 sheets) and hub guides (2 sheets) for Week 1, or replace lost copies later. Do not print the entire workbook for every child.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 6 character blanks and 12 arm strips: 4 teams + 2 mentor examples.
- [ ] 6 simple hinges or axle assemblies; use tape hinges if parts are limited.
- [ ] 2 clear dance areas, sized after rehearsing the complete routine.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 3 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W03_dance.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Open W03_dance.py. Test a small pivot of 90 wheel degrees before class. Use low speed and a clear perimeter. The turn helper is an open-loop pivot, not a command for a 90-degree heading change. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] The tested character may ride on the stopped base after a mentor clearance check. Its mechanism is passive; the robot program makes the base dance.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
