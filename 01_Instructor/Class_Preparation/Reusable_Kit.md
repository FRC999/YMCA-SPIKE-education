# Reusable class kit

These are packing targets for 12 children, not a claim that items are owned. Check what you have, pack it, and buy or borrow only the remaining gaps.

- [ ] 2 assembled SPIKE bases and 2 fully charged hubs, labeled A and B.
- [ ] 4 laptops and 4 chargers: 2 robot control, 2 build references. Two laptops still work with printed build cards.
- [ ] 2 tested USB data cables plus 1 compatible spare; 2 hub charging leads/supplies as appropriate to the actual kit.
- [ ] 4 labeled team trays and 4 equal build packs; keep required chassis parts out of these packs.
- [ ] 4 mounting/clearance templates traced from the actual base; 2 tested mentor examples.
- [ ] 14 sharpened pencils and 14 erasers (12 children + 2 spares); 2 sharpeners.
- [ ] 4 rulers, 2 measuring tapes, 2 rolls removable floor-safe tape and 2 rolls craft tape.
- [ ] 2 pairs child-safe scissors at supervised build tables; pre-cut most card before class.
- [ ] 4 clipboards or firm writing surfaces, 4 labeled storage bags and 1 folder for completed records.
- [ ] 2 visible timers and 4 table labels: Robot A, Build A, Robot B, Build B.
- [ ] 1 instructor display/projector setup if the room requires it; load slides and files offline.
- [ ] 4–6 student instructors assigned by name and 1 adult lead; confirm YMCA supervision requirements.

## Print once and reuse

- [ ] Role cards: print TWO copies of both pages of [Role_Cards.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Role_Cards.pdf), single-sided. Four sheets make 12 cards: two robot sets and two build sets. Cut and place one set at each station.
- [ ] Hub guide: TWO copies of [SPIKE_Hub_Color_Guide.pdf](../../06_References/SPIKE_Hub_Color_Guide.pdf), one at each robot. Color is useful; the printed names and patterns still work in black and white.
- [ ] Hand-label four table signs and four team trays T1–T4.

## Room and build preparation

Keep two robot lanes clear. Place each build table away from the lane with children facing their parts. Prepare a lightweight mentor model for each robot before the first team arrives. Divide spare LEGO parts fairly; card and tape are the planned fallback. Trace the real robot and approved mounting area for the four templates; do not assume an untested attachment will fit.
