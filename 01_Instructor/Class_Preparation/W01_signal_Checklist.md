# Week 1 print and bring checklist

Meet your robot. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **5 copies** of [W01_signal_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W01_signal_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W01_signal_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W01_signal_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [W01_signal_Checklist.pdf](W01_signal_Checklist.pdf) — lead packing checklist; optional if checked digitally.

Required student/record sheets: **10 pages**. Add four role-card sheets and two hub guides: **16 class sheets total**. The [class packet](../../02_Student/Printouts/W01_Class_Print_Packet.pdf) includes these exact quantities; print one copy. One lead checklist and speaker-note copy are optional. Do not print an opening assessment or a full workbook for every child.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 6 pre-cut card faces or name flags: 4 teams + 2 mentor examples.
- [ ] 12 card brace strips: 2 for each of the six models.
- [ ] 1 box of 12 crayons or markers for team names.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 1 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W01_signal.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Bring the separate EV3 R3PTAR, its charged power supply and cardboard target ONLY if its autonomous demonstration has passed rehearsal. Keep it out of student lanes.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Keep wheels raised for the first signal run. Open W01_signal.py in a SPIKE Prime Python project. Confirm the A/B motor mapping with the mentor smoke test. Welcome children with names and mentor introductions, then demonstrate the robots. Use no opening quiz or ability rating; prior experience was already asked about. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] Fit the approved mascot to the stopped base for the retest. The first robot teams use a mentor example or the bare base.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
