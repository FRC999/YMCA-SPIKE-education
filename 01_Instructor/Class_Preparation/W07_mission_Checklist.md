# Week 7 print and bring checklist

Community robot mission. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **5 copies** of [W07_mission_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W07_mission_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W07_mission_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W07_mission_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [W07_mission_Checklist.pdf](W07_mission_Checklist.pdf) — lead packing checklist; optional if checked digitally.

Required student/record sheets: **10 pages**. Add one optional packing checklist and one optional lead speaker-note copy. Print the reusable role cards (4 sheets) and hub guides (2 sheets) for Week 1, or replace lost copies later. Do not print the entire workbook for every child.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 4 saved team attachments or scenes and 2 mentor example courses.
- [ ] 8 spare card panels for four team scene improvements.
- [ ] 6 lightweight cubes and 2 wall/marker courses matching the chosen missions.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 7 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W07_mission.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Keep the same base and ports. Offer two core routes: supply delivery to a marker or careful parking near a shelf. Open W07_mission.py. Reserve a marked 100 × 100 cm area per kit. Each team gets its own saved recipe. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] The robot uses one familiar tested mission. T1/T3 can test with mentor course pieces before their own scene is built. Combine the approved scene and program in the retest.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
