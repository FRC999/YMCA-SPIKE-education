# Week 5 print and bring checklist

The floor tells the robot. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **5 copies** of [W05_marker_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W05_marker_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W05_marker_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W05_marker_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [W05_marker_Checklist.pdf](W05_marker_Checklist.pdf) — lead packing checklist; optional if checked digitally.

Required student/record sheets: **10 pages**. Add one optional packing checklist and one optional lead speaker-note copy. Print the reusable role cards (4 sheets) and hub guides (2 sheets) for Week 1, or replace lost copies later. Do not print the entire workbook for every child.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 12 light card road tiles, 6 flat dark bands and 6 side platforms.
- [ ] Each band starts at least 5 cm wide; increase width if the rehearsed step requires it.
- [ ] 2 downward color sensors on D; 1 roll of matte dark tape for repairs.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 5 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W05_marker.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Mount sensor roughly 1–2 cm above the surface as a starting setup, then bench-adjust until bright and dark readings clearly differ. Open W05_marker.py. Put the marker about 25 cm ahead of the sensor starting position. Retest under room lighting. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] Robot teams measure the actual bright/dark readings. Use those measurements to choose the threshold; a colored decoration is not automatically a reliable marker.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
