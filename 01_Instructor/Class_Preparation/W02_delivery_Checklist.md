# Week 2 print and bring checklist

Delivery to a target. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print for this session

- [ ] **5 copies** of [W02_delivery_Team_Sheet.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W02_delivery_Team_Sheet.pdf) — 4 team sheets + 1 spare; share one sheet per team.
- [ ] **4 copies** of [W02_delivery_Build_Card.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/W02_delivery_Build_Card.pdf) — 1 per team; one page each.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [Attendance.pdf](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) — 12 rows, one class record.
- [ ] **1 copies** of [W02_delivery_Checklist.pdf](W02_delivery_Checklist.pdf) — lead packing checklist; optional if checked digitally.

Required student/record sheets: **10 pages**. Add one optional packing checklist and one optional lead speaker-note copy. Print the reusable role cards (4 sheets) and hub guides (2 sheets) for Week 1, or replace lost copies later. Do not print the entire workbook for every child.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] 14 pencils, 14 erasers, 4 rulers, 4 team trays/build packs, 4 laptops/chargers, 2 robot kits, 2 timers and the tested cables are packed.

## This week’s additional supplies

- [ ] 6 card sheets for trays: 4 team trays + 2 mentor trays.
- [ ] 6 lightweight cubes: 4 for build testing + 1 at each robot.
- [ ] 2 floor targets, each 40 cm from the tested front-edge start.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 2 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W02_delivery.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Open W02_delivery.py. Set a 100 cm clear lane with a 40 cm goal and extra stopping space. Check front-edge start marks. Use 180, then 360 wheel degrees as trial values. Keep SPEED at 200. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] A mentor supplies an example tray for the first robot turn. Keep payload and tray unchanged during each distance comparison; test the team tray during the retest.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
