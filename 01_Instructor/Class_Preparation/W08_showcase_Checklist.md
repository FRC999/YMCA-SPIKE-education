# Week 8 showcase checklist

Robot showcase. Prepare for 12 children in teams T1–T4.

## Print once

Download the [58-page timed delivery showcase packet](../../02_Student/Printouts/W08_Class_Print_Packet.pdf). Print one copy on US Letter, Actual Size (100%), single-sided, with automatic page orientation.

| Packet pages | Use |
|---|---|
| 1 | [One-page mentor checklist](W08_showcase_Checklist.pdf) |
| 2 | Course assembly diagram and 5 cm scale check |
| 3–18 | Kit A course, rows A–H, two tiles per row |
| 19–34 | Kit B course, rows A–H, two tiles per row |
| 35–39 | [Timed showcase team sheets](../../02_Student/Printouts/W08_showcase_Timed_Team_Sheet.pdf), four teams and one spare |
| 40–43 | [Build cards](../../02_Student/Printouts/W08_showcase_Build_Card.pdf), one per team |
| 44–57 | [Final checks](../../02_Student/Printouts/Final_Check.pdf), 12 individual copies and two spares |
| 58 | [Attendance](../../02_Student/Printouts/Attendance.pdf) |

If both assembled Week 7 courses are ready, print pages **1–2 and 35–58**. If only one can be reused, omit its tile range. The assembly guide on page 2 uses the [standalone Week 7 PDF](../../02_Student/Printouts/W07_Speed_Trial_Full_Size_Course.pdf) page numbers. Use the table above when printing the Week 8 packet. Reuse role cards and hub guides.

## Bring the reusable kit

- [ ] Check every item in [Reusable_Kit.md](Reusable_Kit.md).
- [ ] Pack 14 pencils, 14 erasers, four rulers, four laptops and chargers, two robot kits, two stopwatches, a rotation timer and tested cables.
- [ ] Bring the four saved exhibits and projects with offline copies, Week 7 trial sheets, recorded kit settings, trays or cradles and cubes. Pack four repair packs, six sign blanks and eight card strips.

## Before children arrive

- [ ] Set up both 50 × 160 cm courses. For new tiles, check the 5 cm ruler, trim all four edges and join rows A–H with L on the left facing FINISH. Match the ticks and tape seams from behind. Tape both courses flat and verify 60 cm from START to FINISH, 90 cm of printed runout and clear floor at both ends.
- [ ] Pair the correct hub, check left motor A and right motor B, and verify Stop on both kits. Open each team's saved Week 7 program. Match it to the same kit, travel setting, approved speed, tray and cube used for its 3/3 valid set. T1/T2 normally use Kit A and T3/T4 use Kit B.
- [ ] Rehearse each saved configuration on the floor. Align the fixed chassis mark at START. Start the stopwatch at first movement after the ready pause. Stop when that same mark crosses FINISH. Record tenths and watch the robot through its full stop to judge cargo, lane and runout.
- [ ] If a configuration fails rehearsal, use the team's previously verified timed setup if it passes a fresh floor check. If a live run fails or cannot start, show the saved program, cradle and Week 7 evidence within the four-minute slot.
- [ ] Confirm visitors with YMCA staff. Open the [Week 8 slides](../../03_Presentations/W08_showcase.pptx) and [speaker notes](../Speaker_Notes/W08_showcase_Speaker_Notes.md). Assign two robot mentors, two build mentors and one timekeeper per course. Alternate runs if the lanes cannot be supervised independently.
- [ ] Distribute the individual check before coaching. Allow six minutes per team to rehearse. For each four-minute presentation, use 0:00–0:45 to explain, 0:45–2:00 to run and report, 2:00–3:00 for one question and 3:00–4:00 to reset.

## At the end

- [ ] Save four team projects and store four labeled builds.
- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in [SESSION_PREP.md](../../SESSION_PREP.md). Keep completed child records in the approved private system.
