## Before children arrive

- [ ] Open the Week 8 slide PDF/PPTX, speaker notes, complete W08_showcase.py and build references. Save offline copies.
- [ ] Run the week’s robot sequence on BOTH bases; confirm intended hub, ports, stop controls and save workflow.
- [ ] Confirm any visitors with YMCA staff. Load team projects and inspect lanes before arrival. Use W08_showcase.py only as a clean fallback; normally reopen the team’s week 7 program. No last-minute firmware update. Prepare two mentor examples and four labeled team build packs. Rehearse attachment clearance, a hand test and a quick swap. Show the hub color guide.
- [ ] This is finishing and presentation time, not a new complex mechanism. Each team gets a six-minute robot rehearsal and a four-minute demonstration.
- [ ] Assign two robot mentors and two build mentors. Use additional mentors for transitions or support. Keep paper recording short and at the robot.

## At the end

- [ ] Collect team sheets and individual checks, count supplies, stop hubs and assign charging.
- [ ] Record shortages or failed tests in SESSION_PREP.md. Keep completed child records in the approved private system, not in this public repository.
