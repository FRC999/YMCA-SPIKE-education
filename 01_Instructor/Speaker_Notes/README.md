The baseline remains eight students, two kits, two pod mentors and an adult lead. Weeks 1–7 use the normal two-rotation schedule; Week 8 has its own assessment, rehearsal and demonstration schedule. Confirm actual enrollment and staffing before assigning groups.

Week 1 reuses the existing detailed script and adds a printable preparation page. Its [HTML companion](../W01_Detailed_Speaker_Notes.html) and revised deck remain available. The new Weeks 2–8 companions expand the shorter embedded notes; their presentations were not reauthored in this update. DOCX is the editorial source and Markdown is generated by [the document sync script](../../scripts/sync_documents.py). Keep companion content and affected slides/programs aligned during future changes.

Use the [instructor guide](../Instructor_and_Mentor_Guide.md) for broader setup and assessment guidance, the [student workbook](../../02_Student/Student_Workbook.md) for student records, and the [prep tracker](../../SESSION_PREP.md) for actual hardware status. Rehearse each program on both physical kits before teaching; document generation is not a robot test.
