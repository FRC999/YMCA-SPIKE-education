# Week 4 build card

## A garage with a dependable target wall

Team ____  Builders A ____ B ____ C ____

Build a stable garage entrance and a broad, flat back wall that the distance sensor can face.

**Materials:** Pre-cut cardboard, tape or spare bricks, ruler and a small wheeled proxy or footprint template.

## Try it

1. Make a bay wider than the base footprint.
2. Brace the flat back wall so a light tap does not tip it over.
3. Move the unpowered proxy through the entrance. Mark a stopping zone in front of the wall.

## Our jobs

Builder connects pieces. Designer chooses/finds parts and checks the plan. Tester performs the hand test and reports one result. Everyone builds. Rotate when the mentor calls time.

## Test and improve

Proxy clears the entrance; target wall stays flat and upright at sensor height.

We noticed ____________________. We changed ____________________.

Use the mentor wall during initial parking tests. The mentor approves the team garage before its robot retest; keep the broad wall and sensor line of sight clear.
