# Week 1 build card

## A removable robot mascot

Team ____  Builders A ____ B ____ C ____

Make a name flag or face that stays upright and attaches without covering a wheel, port, button or cable.

**Materials:** Small Technic beams and pins, pre-cut card, crayons, removable tape and a mentor-made mounting template.

## Try it

1. Choose a team name and lay out a lightweight mast or face.
2. Build a wide or braced support using the mounting template.
3. Gently tilt and tap the model by hand. Improve one weak connection.

## Our jobs

Builder connects pieces. Designer chooses/finds parts and checks the plan. Tester performs the hand test and reports one result. Everyone builds. Rotate when the mentor calls time.

## Test and improve

Stands after a gentle tap; stays inside the clearance template; no loose parts.

We noticed ____________________. We changed ____________________.

Fit the approved mascot to the stopped base for the retest. The first robot teams use a mentor example or the bare base.

Fit attachments only to a stopped robot after the mentor checks clearance. Leave wheels, buttons, cables and sensors clear.
