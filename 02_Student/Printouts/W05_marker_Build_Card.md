# Week 5 build card

## A road with a delivery marker

Team ____  Builders A ____ B ____ C ____

Build a flat road section with one clear dark stopping band and a small delivery platform beside it.

**Materials:** Light card floor tiles, dark tape, tape tabs, ruler and a base footprint template.

## Try it

1. Join flat tiles without raised edges in the wheel path.
2. Add one wide dark band across the sensor path and a delivery platform off the lane.
3. Slide the footprint template through the route and remove catches or loose tape.

## Our jobs

Builder connects pieces. Designer chooses/finds parts and checks the plan. Tester performs the hand test and reports one result. Everyone builds. Rotate when the mentor calls time.

## Test and improve

Flat route, one visible marker and clear wheel/sensor paths. Mentor verifies the marker is wide enough for the tested movement step.

We noticed ____________________. We changed ____________________.

Robot teams measure the actual bright/dark readings. Use those measurements to choose the threshold; a colored decoration is not automatically a reliable marker.

Fit attachments only to a stopped robot after the mentor checks clearance. Leave wheels, buttons, cables and sensors clear.
