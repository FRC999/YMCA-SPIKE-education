# Week 6 build card

## A rescue scoop or guide

Team ____  Builders A ____ B ____ C ____

Build a removable guide that pushes a cube without trapping it under the robot.

**Materials:** Spare beams, pins or pre-cut folded card, one lightweight cube, mounting template and flat test surface.

## Try it

1. Make a flat bumper and push the cube by hand with the proxy.
2. Add short side guides or change one guiding edge.
3. Compare the two versions with the same cube and hand-test path.

## Our jobs

Builder connects pieces. Designer chooses/finds parts and checks the plan. Tester performs the hand test and reports one result. Everyone builds. Rotate when the mentor calls time.

## Test and improve

Cube moves ahead; guide clears the floor and does not cover the downward sensor.

We noticed ____________________. We changed ____________________.

Each robot station has a mentor sample before the first rotation. Fit approved team guides for retests. Keep motor and sensor mounts unchanged.

Fit attachments only to a stopped robot after the mentor checks clearance. Leave wheels, buttons, cables and sensors clear.
