Hands on build station cards

Print four copies of the current week PDF. Teams build and hand-test while the other team uses the robot.

Week 1 A removable robot mascot

Make a name flag or face that stays upright and attaches without covering a wheel, port, button or cable.

  1. Choose a team name and lay out a lightweight mast or face.
  2. Build a wide or braced support using the mounting template.
  3. Gently tilt and tap the model by hand. Improve one weak connection.

Printable card · Robot test sheet

Week 2 A cargo tray that keeps its load

Build a removable tray or cradle with an edge that keeps the cube aboard.

  1. Choose where the cube will sit and build a flat support.
  2. Add an edge or rail without making the tray touch the floor or wheels.
  3. Tilt the tray gently by hand and compare one change to its edge.

Printable card · Robot test sheet

Week 3 A dancing character with moving parts

Build a lightweight character with arms or a feature that can move by hand and still stays attached.

  1. Build the character and a stable support.
  2. Add a free-moving arm, hinge or axle that stays outside the wheel path.
  3. Move it by hand ten times and improve a loose or tipping part.

Printable card · Robot test sheet

Week 4 A garage with a dependable target wall

Build a stable garage entrance and a broad, flat back wall that the distance sensor can face.

  1. Make a bay wider than the base footprint.
  2. Brace the flat back wall so a light tap does not tip it over.
  3. Move the unpowered proxy through the entrance. Mark a stopping zone in front of the wall.

Printable card · Robot test sheet

Week 5 A road with a delivery marker

Build a flat road section with one clear dark stopping band and a small delivery platform beside it.

  1. Join flat tiles without raised edges in the wheel path.
  2. Add one wide dark band across the sensor path and a delivery platform off the lane.
  3. Slide the footprint template through the route and remove catches or loose tape.

Printable card · Robot test sheet

Week 6 A rescue scoop or guide

Build a removable guide that pushes a cube without trapping it under the robot.

  1. Make a flat bumper and push the cube by hand with the proxy.
  2. Add short side guides or change one guiding edge.
  3. Compare the two versions with the same cube and hand-test path.

Printable card · Robot test sheet

Week 7 A small community mission scene

Build a small scene or improve an attachment for a chosen community job.

  1. Choose a user and one job: deliver to a floor marker or stop near a garage wall.
  2. Build the matching destination, useful attachment or model of the user’s need.
  3. Hand-test the scene, identify one weak feature and improve it.

Printable card · Robot test sheet

Week 8 A working exhibit for the showcase

Finish a stable exhibit that helps visitors understand the team’s robot job.

  1. Repair or strengthen one loose part of the saved build.
  2. Add a small sign or model showing the user and useful job.
  3. Rehearse three short speaking parts while the robot is with the other team.
