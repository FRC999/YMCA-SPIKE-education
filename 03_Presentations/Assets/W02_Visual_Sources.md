# Week 2 visual sources

W02_motor_concept.png is an original AI-generated conceptual illustration created for this lesson on November 7, 2026 and accepted by Alex. It depicts a generic motor, not a cutaway of a LEGO motor or a wiring/build instruction. It omits the hub, switching and other internal components. Preserve its proportions on slides.

The turn fractions, radius/diameter and rolling-distance diagrams are editable teaching diagrams. The rolling illustration compares start and finish positions of one wheel. The numerical wheel size is illustrative; measure both classroom kits.

Teaching references, checked November 7, 2026:

- [LEGO medium angular motor](https://education.lego.com/en-us/products/lego-technic-medium-angular-motor/45603/): integrated rotation feedback.
- [Exploratorium: Magnet + Electricity = Motor](https://annex.exploratorium.edu/xref/exhibits/magnet_electricity_motor.html): current in a coil and magnetic force produce motion.
- [LEGO Going the Distance](https://education.lego.com/en-gb/lessons/prime-extra-resources/going-the-distance/): circumference, a 5.6 cm diameter example and 17.6 cm per turn.
